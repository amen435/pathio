// POWERED BY MCP

What is MCP?
The nervous system.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI agents connect to real tools and services — like browsers, email, databases, and apps — so they can take real actions in the world, not just generate text. Think of MCP as the bridge between Pathio's AI brain and everything it needs to act on your behalf.
